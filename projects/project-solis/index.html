Introducing Project Solis

American made. Future focused.

A new chapter for Gallup. Our proposed advanced-manufacturing campus brings solar cells and modules, onsite power, and reclaimed process water together in New Mexico.

The campus is planned for the production of solar cells, solar modules and solar glass, with up to 1 GW of behind-the-meter power generation.

Development remains subject to financing, engineering, permitting, and approvals.
